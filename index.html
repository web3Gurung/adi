FAQ

Common Questions

How often should I visit the dentist?
Every 6 months for a routine check-up and cleaning. Patients with ongoing treatment may need more frequent visits as advised.
Is root canal treatment painful?
No. Modern single-sitting root canal treatment is performed under local anaesthesia. Most patients feel little to no discomfort during the procedure.
Do you accept CGHS / ECHS patients?
Yes. We are empanelled with CGHS, ECHS, DJB, BSES, MCD, DTC, Delhi University, and Delhi Police. Please carry your card on your visit.
What age should children start visiting?
We recommend the first dental visit by age 1, or within 6 months of the first tooth appearing. Early visits help build positive habits and catch issues early.
How long does implant treatment take?
The full process typically takes 3–6 months depending on bone health and healing. We will walk you through a personalised timeline at your consultation.
Dental Emergency? Call us immediately — same-day emergency slots available.
